# Body sheet captures (retired)

The `body_sheet` binary these came from was retired on 2026-09-15, under the
[isomere plan](../../../mesocosm/design_docs/2026-09-15_isomere_plan.md)'s M5.
The rationale — what it was, why a second GUI toolkit existed, what replaced
each capability, and what was deliberately not carried over — is in
[the genet document host plan](../../design_docs/2026-09-13_genet_document_host_plan.md),
under "Retiring the body sheet".

The three PNGs under `run-1788766069347/` are the reviewed live-equipment
captures from 2026-09-06: a carried dressing, the same dressing attached to a
part, and it detached again. They are kept as the last evidence of that
receipt; nothing regenerates them. The equivalent surface is now the `session`
host's sheet and equipment panels, covered by `testing/session/`.
