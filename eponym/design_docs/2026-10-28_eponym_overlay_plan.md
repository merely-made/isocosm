# Eponym overlay: driving on Isocosm and the played loop

**Status:** 2026-10-28: cut from `2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md` and
`2026-07-30_paredros_founding_plan.md`, which are archived in `archive_docs/2026-10-28/`,
and from the open items of `DOC_README.md`. Built: the Eponym side of the overlay contract
(`shared/isocosm-overlay/src/eponym/`: act, actuation, agree, ask, checkpoint, handoff), and the
E2 families moved onto native Isocosm (`eponym-world`, `eponym-social` and `eponym-identity`
no longer exist; `eponym/crates/` holds `eponym-client`, `-motion`, `-play`, `-sortie`).
Open: E3 (driving on Isocosm, with the folded functional-loops and memory lanes), E4 (headed,
two-peer, end to end), and the charter's open design as questions.

**For:** Eponym as a game over the Isocosm sim: E3, E4 and the lanes ruling 801 folded into E3
(session and contact, death and continuation, strike quality, material work, memory and
recall), plus the carried headed findings, save defects and the founding charter's open
questions.

Not here: the sim's schema, processes and families certification (`sim`); arrived-outsider
reserve accounting (`sim`, with E3's body admission gate); body overlap in movement (`bodies`);
view-selection and other speed bars (`sim-execution`); the resident surface, atlas and
atlas-slot work (`place-graph-engine`); glyph canon and journeys (`wing-organs`); the hagiograph's
promotion test (`wing-organs`); the VTT (`vtt-overlay`).

Receipts from this plan's runs go to one file,
`archive_docs/receipts/2026-10-28_eponym_overlay_plan_receipts.md`, never into this plan (DOC_POLICY
§8). Evidence that matters is also a test in the repository or a line in
Findings.

  without waiting for Mesocosm's M3 (798, amending 231), side by side with the VTT. From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:268`, `:288-289`.

  require native capabilities rather than a game-side authority." From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:24`.
- **Done-conditions are draws from a declared space, never fixtures** (ruling 15), and every
  moved file stays within the 600-line ceiling. From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:241-243`.
- **Two players' creatures are peers in one world** (185, 242: two peers in E4). From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:277`.
- **A death ends a life, not the world**: an arriving outsider is native arrival (238).
  From: `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:312`.

## Deferred and parked

- **Work and agreement terms beyond the built keys**: only asked grade, danger, danger-cap and
  offered share are carried, each within `u8`; other keys refuse as unbuilt, duplicate keys and
  overflowing counts refuse; the wider contract vocabulary stays visible but unbuilt. Waits on
  a ruling to widen the vocabulary. Rulings 63, 67. From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:40`.
- **World conditions (surgery and magic)**: parked (`wing-organs` D1). From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:338`.
- **Glyph marks in the scene, a real canon, durable journey persistence**: the scene half is
  `orthographic-voxel-presentation` far marks (L10); canon and journeys are `wing-organs` G3 and
  G4, parked (`wing-organs` D1). From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-25_eponym_overlay_plan.md:464`.
- **Sky creatures, a later scale experiment**: done when "one organism has a coherent coarse
  lifecycle, produces an observable local effect, and responds to one local action without
  duplicating its state on approach or departure." Walking settlements, piloting, a disc world,
  varying gravity, moving habitats and world-supporting organisms each need their own evidence;
  "First prove that one unfamiliar rule makes an ordinary place interesting to inhabit." From:
  `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-07-30_paredros_founding_plan.md:508-531`.
- **Structurally different worlds**: initial rules from a small curated set (for example an
  atmospheric-charge world or a resonant-material world), each changing at least one ordinary
  activity and one dangerous encounter and discoverable before survival depends on guessing;
  products declare support for a world's mechanisms and missing ones get an explicit admission
  result. Illustrative, "not implemented world types." Waits on world conditions (279, see
  `sim`). From: `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-07-30_paredros_founding_plan.md:325-347`.
- **First design fixtures**: the damaged crossing, and "one line, three lives" (crossing keeper,
  marsh surveyor, displaced repairer); later, a tradition before and after a historical
  disruption. From: `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-07-30_paredros_founding_plan.md:480-537`.
