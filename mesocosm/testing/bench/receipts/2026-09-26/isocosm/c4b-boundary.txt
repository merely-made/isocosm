Where prey ran out part way through a pass of hunters under checkpoint 4b's
wider hunting domains, measured before the certified runs, and how often the
crowd would have refused a draw because those hunters were in more than one
state.

How: `boundary_setup2.py` copied the core and probe as they stood just
before e29c338 into a scratch tree and instrumented the copy; they differ from
that commit only in the probe binary's handling of refused draws, which the
measurer does not use, and the default fat, which it sets explicitly. Its core logs
every weighted target search: the tick, the actor's place, the process,
whether a target was found, and what the actor holds. A pass at a site where
some hunters found prey and some found none is a shortage. Its crowd logs
each shortage with the number of hunting states there, and where the real
crowd would refuse (more than one state) it feeds the first hunters in bin
order so the run goes on. `boundary-measure-4b.rs` draws worlds and dynamics
seeds as the probe binary does (master seed 20260930, 1,000 draws) and runs
the exact runner and three crowds on each. "More than one state" counts the
shortages the crowd refuses.

Four domains were measured. Each widens checkpoint 4's (hunters 4-16 per
site, bites 1-2, appetites 3-6, no fat) at both ends:

  A  hunters 12-48, bites 2-6, appetites 2-12, fat 0-24, food
  B  hunters  8-32, bites 2-4, appetites 2-10, fat 0-32, food
  C  hunters 12-48, bites 2-6, appetites 2-12, fat 0-48, food
  C  the same with water

Shortage events (draws with one; events among hunters in more than one state)

             exact            crowd            averaged           unweighted
  A food     97 (41; 0)       89 (38; 2)      329 (138; 7)       66 (32; 0)
  B food      5  (3; 0)        3  (2; 0)       24  (15; 0)        2  (1; 0)
  C food      2  (1; 0)        1  (1; 0)       67  (29; 3)        0  (0; 0)
  C water     0  (0; 0)        1  (1; 0)       24  (16; 0)        0  (0; 0)

Domain C is the one certified. With fat up to 24 (A), prey that starve out of
their competitions leave too little behind for the hunters at many sites, and
after a first shortage has split a site's hunters into the fed and the unfed,
a second can find both hungry: the crowd would have refused 2 draws in 1,000
and the averaged crowd 7. Fat up to 48 (C) keeps A's hunters, bites and
appetites while leaving prey enough to eat nearly everywhere. B, narrower at
every end, shorts as rarely as C. In C only the averaged crowd, the negative
control, which starves prey hardest, met unlike hunters short of prey (3
draws in 1,000 with food). A refused draw is kept on record in the receipt
and left out of that arm's comparisons; in the certified runs no arm refused
a draw.
