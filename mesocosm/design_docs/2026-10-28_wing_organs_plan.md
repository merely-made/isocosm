# Wing organs: the hagioglyph and the impresa

**Status:** 2026-10-28: cut from `2026-09-26_wing_organs_plan.md` and `2026-09-15_glyph_expression_plan.md`, which are archived in
`archive_docs/2026-10-28/`. Kernels exist and are shared (`shared/wing-glyphs`: canon, journey, divine period accumulator, expression table, pack; eidetic's `impresa`: record type and kinds); effects are native (`shared/isocosm/src/effects/`). Every phase below is parked under ruling 279; nothing here is in progress.

**For:** the two wing organs that no single game owns: the **hagioglyph** (divinity: the glyph canon and its revisions, expression through traits, experience, the journey, ascension, the chosen referent and its periods, chains and standing) and the **impresa** (association: records of what a thing has come to be associated with, read as fact, belief or legend). It also holds the one owed ruling on what "parked" means (ruling 673 against the later design in 404 to 407, 801 and 1009).

Not here: the unpark condition for ruling 279 and its measured state (`sim`, parked items); how a magic system is composed, priced and cast, and effects-rendering remainders (`functional-generation`); the body contract and condition carryover into a new life (`wing-phenotype-contract`, wing.4 there); glyph display on far marks (`orthographic-voxel-presentation` L10); deep time and the hagiograph's history (`sim` S5); Eponym's glyph reading and invocation (`eponym-overlay`).

Receipts from this plan's runs go to `archive_docs/<date>/`, never into this
plan (DOC_POLICY §8). Evidence that matters is a test in the repository or a

## Constraints

- **Parked under 279.** Divinity and ascension stay ruled and pull no code lane until the condition recorded in `sim` holds. Doctrine Organs 1 (ascension sacrifices, grades), Organs 2 (power is the frequency of the domain's process), Organs 3 (peeking behind the curtain) and World 9 (divine places and alignment) are "a design to resume from, not one to build to" (`design_docs/doctrine.md`, Organs and World 9). Source: ruling 279; ruling 673.
- **Ruling 673 allows moving existing code and no new design on the parked rungs.** Mark chose "Move as code; rungs stay parked (Recommended)" (design record `design_docs/2026-09-18_wing_design_plan.md`, ruling 673). The moved code is `shared/isocosm/src/effects/`, `eponym/crates/eponym-play/src/glyphs.rs` and `mesocosm/crates/mesocosm-runtime/src/glyphs.rs`. What 673 means for design already written is the owed ruling in Questions.
- **Effect IDs alone are not implemented effects.** Each needs behaviour, cost, target rule, receiver interaction and visible explanation. Source: wing organs plan §1, "World vocabulary and effect identity".
- **Reopening never reruns a newer generator over an inhabited world's meanings.** The canon correspondence and the expression table are authored, may be shuffled by seed once, and are then saved. Canon changes during play need explicit migration of completion and correspondence and must not quietly revoke a god or complete an old collection under new meanings. Mark, 2026-09-15: "Authored expression with a pinned shuffle."
- **Canon revision is one-way.** A hagiograph promotion is one cause of a canon revision; the divinity organ consumes the memorial organ and never the reverse. Every acquisition keeps the revision it was accepted under; completion and the ascension basis are judged against the journey's founding canon; only the live effect of an owned glyph follows the current revision (G5, Mark, 2026-09-14).
- **The journey is append-ordered and unrewritable.** Accepted append order is authoritative; reacquisition never rewrites the first acquisition; `ascension_basis()` keeps the exact grant prefix at first ascension; a rejected grant leaves evidence intact and reports exhaustion rather than evicting an original acquisition.
- **No hidden bonuses.** Interest, decay, averaging, caps, stockpiles, spending, ongoing access during a period, chain caps and side-effect routing are explicit world policy. The period ledger takes accepted observations only; it never samples a renderer, a wall clock or an arbitrary biography. Glyph colour, animation and generated layout cannot award an acquisition.
- **Impresa rules.** Every record cites an accepted event and nothing is inferred from a name. Disassociation is a record, never a deletion. The three readings (fact, belief, legend) are of one record and none is a second authority. A record cited by a later accepted event, a god's ascension basis or a faction's stance is never compacted. The Nemesis boundary stands: remembered grudges are fine, rival promotion hierarchies are not. Mark, 2026-09-15: "A god's associations are their impresa, but other things could have theirs too."
- **Experience conditions are general predicates over the log**, not per-glyph mechanics (Mark, 2026-09-15; quoted in G9). A deed must be rare by construction, not by threshold: constant acts are statistics that gate events and are not themselves world events (Mark, 2026-09-16, E2).
- **Journey stays authoritative; the impresa record is parallel.** Ownership, completion and ascension read the journey; the impresa record is emitted alongside, citing the same cause receipt, for other subjects and vessels (Mark, 2026-09-16, scope ruling 2).

### G6. Chains and standing
Effects compose into chains. Standing is a per-individual, per-glyph pair of ledgers, affinity and resistance, moved only by accepted events under stated decay policies. A chain is a typed admission (compatible by behaviour kind and receiver class, length a configured bound). A link's success reads affinity minus resistance and chain length, drawn from the seeded integer stream; a failure is an accepted event and raises resistance, which is capped, decays by a per-world policy and is symmetric (it also defends against that glyph from outside). Affinity raises magnitude, including self-targeted side effects. Decay is evaluated lazily from the last event tick. Products adjudicate; the kernel owns the chain grammar and receipts. Standing first lands as affinity only (Mark, 2026-09-16); resistance and chains "wait for invocation, which does not exist in Mesocosm and is Paredros's ground first."
- Done when: "a two-link chain on compatible declarations admits and an incompatible one refuses by name; success, failure and the resulting resistance change replay identically from a restored save; resistance decays to its floor on a stated policy and blocks an incoming effect of the same glyph in the meantime; a high-affinity invocation reports its declared side effect on the invoker."
- Needs: invocation, which no product has; `eponym-overlay` as the old plan's first ground for it; ruling 279 unpark
- Rulings: 279, 673
- From: `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-26_wing_organs_plan.md:119-143` (G6), `:182` (done-when); `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-15_glyph_expression_plan.md:915-935` (scope rulings 2026-09-16)

### G9. Experience
Mark, 2026-09-15: "Experience conditions are general predicates over the log, not per-glyph mechanics. The form is a conjunction: an encounter with the glyph (it was found, seen inscribed, carried, or embodied) and a deed recorded among the significant events that matches the glyph's meaning. 'If you have killed something with fire and you found this glyph, you have experienced it,' rather than 'set something marked with this glyph on fire.' The trigger is recorded with the manner that satisfied it: discovery, craft, embodiment or deed, and that manner is what shapes the divinity later." Manner maps to provenance kind (embodiment is Trait, deed is Event, craft is Technique, discovery is Custom "glyph:discovery"). Move order: (1) the experience vocabulary in `wing-glyphs`; (2) the manner mapping and a `motif()` test; (3) Mesocosm resolution of deed verbs and encounters; (4) a runtime evaluator, where `absorb` and `absorb_uptake` become the deed feed and `GlyphRules.grants` retires; (5) the example; (6) probe fields; (7) a scenario. A deed has two sources: occasions (rare events: severed, died, grafted, later inherited, expressed, speciated) and feats (a reading that took the record, as a `(Feat, Scale)` pair). Scope: the impresa record is founded first and standing is affinity only (rulings 2026-09-16). Not built: no `experience` module in `wing-glyphs`, no manner mapping.
- Done when: "1. wing-glyphs `experience`: JSON round-trip; `covers` accepts a canon whose every base has a condition and refuses one that does not, and refuses a table whose `canon_revision` disagrees; an empty encounter list refuses at `new`; an unavailable encounter kind round-trips and is refused at evaluation with its reason, never silently unsatisfied; identifiers and bounded text match `canon.rs`'s rules. 2. Manner: each of the four maps to its `ProvenanceKind`; a journey carrying two manners returns two `motif()` groups where a one-manner journey returns one; a grant under a revision older than the founding one is refused. 3. mesocosm-core `experience`: each deed verb matches exactly its `Event` variant and no other; a below-floor deed (`removed == 0`, `mass_mg == 0`) and a disagreeing qualifier do not match; the encounter reading is `&`-only and byte-identical run twice over one unchanged phenotype. 4. mesocosm-runtime: a deed with no encounter grants nothing and leaves the journey byte-identical; the same deed with an encounter acquires once and records thereafter; the same pair retried is a duplicate; a core-rejected carve yields no grant and no mark." Condition 3 names `mesocosm-core`, which no longer exists, and the old scenario and pixel gates named the deleted bench; both owe re-expression in the native crates before this phase starts.
- Needs: ruling 279 unpark; G7 (the impresa record type, founded first); the hagiograph pre-history and feat rule (Deferred); G8 for the expression table the encounter reads
- Rulings: 279, 673, 618
- From: `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-09-15_glyph_expression_plan.md:567-583`, `:631-913` (§9), `:915-935`
