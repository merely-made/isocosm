## 1. Three levels

Ruling 277 gives a body three levels. **Organ systems**, integumentary,
respiratory, circulatory, digestive, nervous, reproductive, the senses, and
systems fauna lack, are assembled from **functions**, and **parts** are
shapes expressing functions (276, 338). The generator riffs new systems by
mixing functions, so new-looking life stays biologically reasonable. A
process reads a body through its parts: it requires a live part expressing
a function and binds the lowest-numbered one (338).

## 2. The function catalogue

One catalogue every game reads (278), holding all fifteen functions of
ruling 277 (461): the five in use, contract, intake, sense, fix and
secrete, and support, conduct, gate, store, circulate, respire, excrete,
reproduce, grip and adhesion. Each lists the shapes that admit it, its
seeding, Grown or Acquired (341), and the mechanic that reads it, or none
yet. The other vocabularies fold in: wing-functions' charge routing maps
its store and gate onto the catalogue's, its actuator onto contract and its
source onto a function §6 settles; Eponym's grip and adhesion are the
catalogue's, their reach measured by the sim (462) and their load declared
until a mechanic measures it; the contact probe's impairments of grip and
reach become harms to the parts that grip and reach. *Reading, not ruled:*
the mapping above, and that a function no mechanic reads is still listed,
marked unread.

## 3. The part

A part has one of the eight shapes (276), the functions it expresses, its
half-extents, where it attaches to its parent (462), its cells allocated
among its functions, its tissue (459), its traits and whether it is
severed. Its capacity and the mass of one cell are read from its extents by
Mesocosm's rule (460): along each axis one cell per two voxels of
half-extent plus one, at most four an axis and 64 in all, each cell
weighing the part's adult mass over its cells, the adult mass being its
voxels priced at the reference mass a segment. *Reading, not ruled:* the
reference mass and segment, 100 mg in 125 voxels, are world rules with
Mesocosm's values as defaults, as 446's reading has the starvation margin,
the ceilings and the routing threshold.

## 4. Where a body's matter sits

Each part holds its own tissue, a mix of nis by provenance, which is the
scruple of ruling 98; the parts that express store hold the body's reserve
(459). A bite lands on one part of its prey, drawn by what each holds, and
incorporation takes a part whole with its matter. This settles 456's item:
Mark's question, whether a meal could depend on the part eaten and where
the reserve is stored, has its answer. *Reading, not ruled:* the meal's
landing, rent and growth keep TD5's one rule; which parts they reach is §6.

## 5. Geometry the sim measures

The sim keeps where each part attaches to its parent and measures what a
mechanic needs from that (462): reach, the farthest contracting part's
distance from the root plus its own extent, as Mesocosm measures it;
clearance and contact as the families that read them arrive. Each mechanic
names the measurement it reads, so an accepted change to the body
invalidates it (405). Pivots, yaw and volumes stay isometer's, for drawing.
