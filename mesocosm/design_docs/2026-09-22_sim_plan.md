**Denizen** (terminology supersession of 2026-09-20; ruling 37): a critter,
of any kingdom and provenance, that the sim remembers individually because
history, relationships or explicit designation makes it matter. It adds to
the critter: notes (§2.8), a disposition on the five factors, inherited
through its lineage and shaped by what it lives, the world's conditions
among it, the first lineages' seeded from the world's disposition (120,
121), event
memory at the high end of the range, relations, and belief-desire-intention
methodology when its fidelity needs it. Notability requires neither
sapience nor a name.

**Sophont** (working word, ruling 45; a naming round is Mark's): a critter
whose memory, naming and tool use reach the top of the range. It adds:
assertion, so it may own, claim a home and property, hold tenets and be
petitioned (53, 54, 50); gear-limited inventory (53); secrets (87); and the
act score of §3.2.

**Character** (ruling 36, 56): a denizen contingent on a group, polity or
collective, even by absence. It adds a rank asserted by a polity and the
obligations that come with it, normative methodology (37), and a system's
reading of the one ledger as its sheet (38). Nothing else is new to the
schema.

**Construct** (ruling 45, 53, 98, 144): provenance made-by-a-maker; may be
a person; contains what it is built to contain; made of the world's nis.
An item can be a sophont by three routes: made so by its maker, awakened by
its story, or inhabited by a mind that moves into it (144). It acts through
its wielder, weighed by the wielder's opinion of it, by its own powers, and
when strong enough by taking its wielder over (145); it is held but never
owned, and it can own things itself (146). Owning any person is slavery,
under a tenet some hold (151).

**Divine** (rulings 42 to 52, 101): provenance intrinsic to the world,
present in every branch after ascendance. Tier from the forms sacrificed;
domain from the means of their acquisition, named freely; power from the
frequency of the domain's process weighted by significance; referent fixed
for the lower tiers and chosen by the greater; presence omnipresent,
reincarnating or fixed; ending only by prophesied conditions asserted at
the ascent. Under ruling 101 the ladder of forms is derived from the gate
axis of the world's magic (§2.7), which is the one change to divinity Mark
allowed, confirmed and extended by ruling 107: the quality of an offering
is the depth of the entity's relation to that magic in its story, so one
magic used religiously grades as high as a wide collection; whether ascent
needs the whole canon or a threshold of potency is a world setting, the
whole canon the favoured default.

**Faction** (rulings 8, 63, 67; D1, D3): a relation, not an agent. Members
are whatever can consent, sophonts or polities; it acts by consent, one
proposing and others joining for the act or for the proposer; its alignment
is its members' weighted by influence (51); it has no state and costs the
scheduler nothing; it is never gated by disagreement, and where it must
decide as one it falls back on its members' preferred way of deciding,
ranked as at a founding and held for that decision (133). A
party is a faction defined by a person; a treaty is a standing agreement
among polities.

**Lineage** (rulings 8, 57, 101): provenance in time, a record and not a
decider. A genotype; a property vector at the far rung (Ptree's
vocabulary); two doors, reproduction rerolling expression and the epoch
boundary changing the genotype in the shop; branching with a plan to grow
and to compete or cooperate. The world has one too.

**Item** (§3.1 of the record; rulings 53, 95, 96, 144; D27): a body
without agency, unless it is a sophont (144); when of note it is a relic
(157), Law A's word. Provenance: maker, materials and the act it
came from. Holding: containment derived, possession asserted. Quality as
affordance: sort, material, make and condition resolve into which
techniques it affords a wielder of a given skill; never a stat. Improved by
a recipe with the item as input, as far as its maker's skill and its story
allow and never capped by its material (143); a thing of note affords
techniques of its own.

**Material** (rulings 96 to 98): nis is matter typed by provenance, kingdom
first and lineage under it, and the world's own kinds under the world;
scruple is a part's measured mix; both are scales of reading, not
materials. A material's properties are its lineage's traits; its
interactions are the effects a nis of that lineage produces and suffers.
There is no material table. A lineage's traits travel between games as
what things are made of, the exception Law A is amended to name (139). The
world's own kinds lie on a spectrum, from a normal base that matches a
ruleset, through a base with variants, to completely generated, set at
founding (140); their interactions are effects, as every lineage's are
(D28).

### 2.7 Magic

A magic system is a trait of the world's genotype, drawn at founding or
grown at a boundary, described on six axes (ruling 101, D30):

| Axis | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Source | the world's fields, the caster's anatomy, a bearer (item, technique, trait), a god, the record's frequency |
| Gate | unconditional; anatomical; acquired on a journey; skill-gated; social, by taboo; placed, by a divine environment |
| Orientation | process-oriented, bending how a process runs; or effect-oriented, yielding a glyph's effect |
| Cost and breach | which account pays; which invariant, if any, may be suspended, at what price, leaving what residue |
| Rhythm | cyclical, continuous, once |
| Manifestation | how it shows and is sensed |

"Bends the rules" is a declared suspension of an invariant at a declared
price; ordinary magic suspends none. Divinity reads any system through
effects, journey and frequency. Origin: suggested from the seed, configured,
static or fluid under conditions (102).

### 3.5 Rulesets

One binding language at two scopes, world-founding and play; both packs
lowered to rulesets and recorded by digest; neither may override the
ledger, the record or the derivation rule (41, §5.1 of the record). The
world scope is the genotype of §2.1. A game's ruleset never reaches the sim;
it reads situations, the ledger and the record, and returns outcomes as
asserted facts and effects on the ledger. Effects entailed by a world's
composition are a later difficulty (41).

Those outcomes are held to ruling 113's test (114). Given a situation with
no player choices, a ruleset reading the ledger as its sheet resolves it with
the same distribution of outcomes as the sim's own background model, checked
on the bench; what players choose is an input, never a seam. So the sim owes
a background outcome model for every kind of outcome a ruleset resolves; the
fight is §3.2's competing instance, where most contests never reach blows
and a blow drains vigour before it wounds (115, 116, 123). A ruleset that
cannot pass the check may still play in a debug or experimental mode, with
a warning (189); a VTT campaign may also switch the sim off (188).

The contract between a game and the sim is ruled in shape (154): a game
submits intents, the sim returns events and a read-only view of each tick,
and outcomes a game settles come back through the handoff and must pass the
sim's invariants. W5 designs the details against it.

### 5.4 Generation on demand

Lifting realises individuals from a cohort's distribution, locations from
wilderness by terrain, nesting steps as locations are generated, and a
teller from a carrier cohort, each conditioned on every asserted fact and
from the seed, so the same place lifts the same way twice (69, 74, 75, 84,
88). Restriction folds them back with the accounts conserved. Nothing
unvisited costs anything.

### 5.5 Similitude, checked on the bench

Three checks the bench (W4) runs on seeded draws, owed by the definitions
and, for what a game resolves, by its ruleset (75, D17, 113, 114):

1. restricting what was just lifted returns the aggregate it came from, a
   consistency check only, since it says nothing of the evolution after;
2. a population run step by step and run as rates ends in the same
   distribution over whatever a later process, a player or the hagiograph
   can read, not only the same means and totals, within a tolerance the
   world's rules state. This is the test (113);
3. a ruleset, reading the ledger as its sheet and given a situation with
   no player choices, resolves it with the same distribution of outcomes as
   the sim's background model, within the same tolerance (114).

Only the fungible are ever run as rates; anything of note runs individually
either way. The lossless runner is the reference each approximation is
checked against, and a world founded exact runs it throughout.

## 6. Sharding, merge and shared worlds

A W2 requirement, not a lane (§4.7 of the record): the sim is sharded by
the place graph, each shard an actor with its own due-event queue, field
passes data-parallel inside a shard, and a deterministic ordered merge of
cross-shard effects per tick designed before the shards are. Time in a
shared world is option A (104, 105): a played trunk, branches merged by
replay, consent to advance past another's foreground, realignment for
branches of different spans, and related worlds by descent or
neighbourhood. Armillary's founding proposal says nothing of determinism or
an ordered merge (checked 2026-09-21); the merge is this plan's to specify
under a lane.
