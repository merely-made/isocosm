### 2.5 Capabilities and knowledge

**Current refinement, 2026-09-28 (407).** Keep three distinct questions:

| Concept | What it answers | What can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability | What current construction and circumstances actually support | maturation, injury, equipment, material or conceptual effects, environmental conditions |
| Repertoire | Which procedures an entity has available to attempt | inherited behavior, discovery, teaching, practice, invention, forgetting |
| Proficiency | How effectively an entity performs a procedure | development, practice, coordination, fatigue and other applicable modifiers |

The existing ability, skill and technique vocabulary below remains useful:
an ability names a supported interaction, skills organize learning and
proficiency, and techniques are procedures with prerequisites. These are not
three aliases for a single level. An infant can possess the structure for
walking before it coordinates walking well; inherited routines need not be
learned from zero. A learned procedure can remain in the repertoire while
injury prevents its execution. Self-belief belongs with perception and belief:
someone may underestimate or overestimate their capability. Decisions read
the actor's understanding; accepted outcomes follow actual circumstances.

**Implementation boundary:** `Entity.skills` currently holds keyed numeric
values, and `rules/body.rs` binds a required function to one live part. Those
are foundations, not the complete representation of these distinctions or
of actions supported by multiple parts and equipment. The storage and
multi-part binding design remain open. Construction declares function;
geometry is queried for named measurements a mechanic requires (405).

**Modifiers, 2026-10-01 (427, 429, 431, 433).** Every modification applies
effects from the world's one vocabulary, and a modifier names its source,
which may be a glyph, a trait, a part, an item, a place, a condition or a
relation (427); every effect the world recognises as fundamental has a glyph
as its manifestation (49), so a canon revision moves what glyphs bind and
leaves other sources alone. Modifiers combine in layers the world's rules
order: a modifier reads conditions and earlier layers only, so no cycle
forms, and within a layer each reading declares its stacking, sum, highest,
lowest, product or replace (429). Amounts stay bounded expression trees
(268). A modifier declares itself distributive, entering each member's state
and splitting a crowd only where its predicate reads members differently, or
collective, the group's own as a polity's accounts are (431). It declares its
scope among bearer, body, relation, group, place and site; adjacency between
pieces on a board is the game's, resolved by its ruleset with positions the
sim never holds (433). An entity's own conditions are §2.3's records (428,
430); what the far rungs show of all this is presentation (432).

### 2.7 Magic

**2026-09-28 refinement (404):** every admitted fundamental effect has a
small cantrip expression and can combine with others at strongly escalating
energy cost. Effect scripts define fundamental behavior and variable
characteristics that can depend on the world's conditions and other
generators' outputs. Both mod-authored and sim-generated magic are intended.
Ruleset spells remain distinctive exemplars; generated spells may extend
their catalogues and reflect the particular world. The
[functional generation plan](2026-09-09_functional_generation_plan.md#composition-design-2026-09-28)
owns composition, exchange and scripting questions. A formula, script runtime,
casting method and CoreRPG/system-specific mappings have not been selected.
This is design work; the existing magic implementation gates remain parked.

A magic system is a trait of the world's genotype, drawn at founding or
grown at a boundary, described on six axes (ruling 101, D30):

| --- | --- |

| Gate | unconditional; anatomical; acquired on a journey; skill-gated; social, by taboo; placed, by a divine environment |

#### Context, evidence and inexpensive interpretation

**Design direction, 2026-09-28.** Shared world facts and events underpin
observations and interpretations; each entity does not need its own copy of
the world or a scan of the complete event log. Section 4's reach model
already separates shared propagation from explicit discoveries. Extend that
boundary with local/indexed candidate information, reusable interpretation
rules and consequential personal differences, including stale information,
mistakes and self-beliefs. Arrival and source matter; missing personal state
must not be interpreted as knowledge of current truth.

Execution ancestry, observed evidence, believed explanation and remembered
significance are distinct. A diagnostic can show which process and conditions
produced an event without making that explanation known to an actor. Broader
claim formation, false allegations, selective recall and dependency indexes
remain design work; the current reach implementation does not certify them.

### 3.5 Rulesets

One binding language at two scopes, world-founding and play; both packs
lowered to rulesets and recorded by digest; neither may override the
ledger, the record or the derivation rule (41, §5.1 of the record). The
world scope is the genotype of §2.1. A game's ruleset never reaches the sim;
it reads situations, the ledger and the record, and returns outcomes as
asserted facts and effects on the ledger. Effects entailed by a world's
composition are a later difficulty (41).

Those outcomes are held to ruling 113's test (114). Given a situation with
no player choices, a ruleset reading the ledger as its sheet resolves it with
the same distribution of outcomes as the sim's own background model, checked
on the bench; what players choose is an input, never a seam. So the sim owes
a background outcome model for every kind of outcome a ruleset resolves; the
fight is §3.2's competing instance, where most contests never reach blows
and a blow drains vigour before it wounds (115, 116, 123). A ruleset that
cannot pass the check may still play in a debug or experimental mode, with
a warning (189); a VTT campaign may also switch the sim off (188).
*Amended 2026-10-03 (538 to 541):* each world chooses whether the sim
leads, rulesets calibrating as above, or its ruleset leads, the sim's
background following it wherever it specifies behaviour; rulesets are
faithful versioned editions with a toolkit grown from them; they mix in
content and subsystems, meeting in an encounter under a named hybrid
profile; and the VTT plays editions while Eponym and Mesocosm keep their
native mechanics.
In a ruleset-led world an edition's own state, hit points or spell slots,
persists beside the body, each fact marked a world fact, native state or a
derived display and their relations declared (542); and unattended, the
ruleset's procedures run with each player's or GM's choice replaced by a
declared decision policy, simplified only as far as the bench shows
outcomes survive (543).

The contract between a game and the sim is ruled in shape (154): a game
submits intents, the sim returns events and a read-only view of each tick,
and outcomes a game settles come back through the handoff and must pass the
sim's invariants. W5 designs the details against it.

### 3.6 Conditions and modifiers

Ruled on 2026-10-01 in the wing design record's one-zoom thread (427 to 431,
433), over 49, 123, 159, 207, 219, 268, 404, 406 and 407. Phase S7 builds it.

**Effects.** The world's rules declare the effects it recognises as
fundamental (49, 404), and each has a glyph as its manifestation (49). A
declaration is written in `wing-glyphs`' format (`EffectDeclaration`:
behaviour, receiver class, cost shape, explanation), since that crate owns
the vocabulary a declaration is written in and products own execution; the
sim executes it. An effect has two parts, either of which may be empty: what
it changes once when applied, in §3.1's typed effects, and what it holds
while it lasts, its modifiers. A burn changes the ledger once; a
fortification holds. *Reading, not ruled:* the two parts are this plan's
reading of 427, since a modification that lasts and one that happens once
must both come from the one vocabulary.

**Readings** are named quantities `meaning` computes, written once for the
individual runner and the crowd (219): mood (227) today, and a skill's
effective level, a computed amount (268), a risk, and a route's travel and
transmission once modifiers reach them. The world's rules declare each
reading and its stacking in each layer: sum, highest, lowest, product or
replace (429).

**Modifiers.** A modifier is an effect's held part, with five declared
properties.

| Property | Values | Ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | glyph, trait, part, item, place, condition or relation; a glyph's effect follows its canon's revision, the others do not | 427 |
| Predicate | §3.1's queries, read against the bearer and its scope | 429 |
| Layer | one of the world's ordered layers; it reads conditions and the layers beneath its own | 429 |
| Scope | bearer, body (its neighbouring tracts), relation, group, place or site; adjacency between pieces on a board is a game's | 433 |
| Aggregate | distributive, entering each member's state, or collective, the group's own | 431 |

A modifier adjusts readings by a bounded expression tree (268). Since no
layer reads a later one, every reading terminates and is computed once per
state, which is what the crowd needs (207). Every modifier's effect has a
glyph, and that glyph is what the far rungs draw (432).

**Conditions** (428, 430). A record of a state a thing's ledger and body do
not hold, such as poisoned, blessed or prone: a key, a magnitude, its cause
and an optional end. A status is a condition with an end. The end is a due
tick on the clock (256), never a countdown, as the record's §3.5 wants. While
a condition holds, its effect's modifiers hold on its bearer. A magnitude
changes only by an effect, as `Ease` eases strain (159). Wounds and vigour
stay the ledger's (123), a severed part the body's; a new life chooses among
conditions and wounds alike (406); the environment's conditions stay on
places (§2.4). *Reading, not ruled:* condition keys are declared by the
world's rules, a ruleset's pack declaring its own (§3.5), and the handoff
refuses an undeclared key, as genesis refuses an undeclared site condition.

A thing holds at most one record per key (436). Each key declares its
stacking from 429's list, and a second application combines magnitudes by
it, the end becoming the later of the two; a key declaring replace takes the
new end with the new magnitude, which is a refresh. A cause is the act that
applied a condition, named by process, tick and place whether or not the act
was noted. A cohort keeps a tally of its members by cause beside its state,
outside the equality `restrict` compares (435): merging adds tallies, and a
lift draws the lifted member's cause from the tally by a seeded draw, after
which that member keeps its own. The members left on either side of a lift
divide the rest of the tally by a seeded draw, since none has an identity
until looked at (113; a reading, not ruled).

**In code, 2026-10-01.** `Entity` keeps no conditions. A `Population`
cohort is "a contiguous identity interval sharing the complete causal
state", and `restrict` merges neighbouring cohorts only when their entities
are equal, so a cause kept inside the record would split cohorts, which 430
forbids. An `Event` is written only for a process marked `note`, and a bulk
application writes one whose subject is the group's representative, so the
history does not name each member's cause either. `Process.glyphs` tags a
process with glyph keys, `meaning::mood` is the one reading computed today,
effect amounts are fixed `u64` until 268's trees land, and the scheduler's
timers (286) can carry ends.

**Forks found writing this, ruled 2026-10-01:** where a crowd member's
condition cause is kept, given those two facts, is a tally beside the cohort
(435); how a second application of a key combines with the first is one
record by the key's stacking (436).

### 5.3 Deep time

The same sim, run forward from the bare world for the founder's span with
nobody attending, so in the aggregate, with checkpoints at epoch boundaries
for replay (93, 91). Catastrophes come from the world's own rules. Mesocosm's
deep time is this today at the scale of one enclosure, ticking the near
rung one idle tick at a time with no checkpoint; both are findings filed
with the isoscape family plan.

**The background at scale (256 to 261, 266, 284, 439 to 441).** The clock
counts the world's unit, a minute by default, each process on its own
period, feeding daily, lineages yearly and polities by season, and the
due-event queue skips idle time, so a century costs evaluations rather than
ticks; an advance's limit guards that work (284). The background is hybrid:
derived where effects commute, and where they do not, the exact crowd runs
wherever grouping pays (261, 266). Grouping stops paying for a site and
lineage when its members per distinct state fall below a bound in the
world's rules, measured at each of its periods; below it the site and
lineage runs its rate model, above it the exact crowd, and every switch is
receipted (439). A rate model is fitted once per rules digest on the bench,
certified against the exact runner as 207's crowd was, and shipped with the
rules, so a world under rules without a certified model stays exact (440).
Until a drawn world lives a century, a cost-only draw with deaths held off
measures a century's evaluations and seconds, labelled cost and never
viability (441).

### 5.4 Generation on demand

Lifting realises individuals from a cohort's distribution, locations from
wilderness by terrain, nesting steps as locations are generated, and a
teller from a carrier cohort, each conditioned on every asserted fact and
from the seed, so the same place lifts the same way twice (69, 74, 75, 84,
88). Restriction folds them back with the accounts conserved. Nothing
unvisited costs anything.

### 5.5 Similitude, checked on the bench

Three checks the bench (W4) runs on seeded draws, owed by the definitions
and, for what a game resolves, by its ruleset (75, D17, 113, 114):

1. restricting what was just lifted returns the aggregate it came from, a
   consistency check only, since it says nothing of the evolution after;
2. a population run step by step and run as rates ends in the same
   distribution over whatever a later process, a player or the hagiograph
   can read, not only the same means and totals, within a tolerance the
   world's rules state. This is the test (113);
3. a ruleset, reading the ledger as its sheet and given a situation with
   no player choices, resolves it with the same distribution of outcomes as
   the sim's background model, within the same tolerance (114).

Only the fungible are ever run as rates; anything of note runs individually
either way. The lossless runner is the reference each approximation is
checked against, and a world founded exact runs it throughout.
