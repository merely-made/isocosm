## Native scope and remaining work

- **wing:allocate-sense:** Allocate living cells to an existing sensory function. Native allocation changes a declared organ's functional cells; it does not grant darkvision, a new modality, a greater sight radius or an edition's spell procedure.
  - **ars5:** Intellego detection reads a specified subject under its guidelines. Allocating native sensory tissue does not implement an Intellego effect, its target or its casting method.
  - **dnd5-srd51:** Darkvision grants a specified kind of sight for a duration; native allocation is anatomical and grants no darkvision semantics. Casting, target restrictions, range and duration remain edition-owned.
  - **pf2-player-core:** The edition grants a named sense with spell-specific duration and heightened behaviour. Native sensing cells are only an anatomical candidate, not that sense or its casting procedure.
- **wing:associate:** Establish or remove a named relationship. A native relation records a fact; loyalty, ownership and membership each need their own rules.
- **wing:birth:** Produce a new individual through a funded reproductive process. Native reproduction accounts for provision and lineage; it does not imply summoning or an existing identity's return.
- **wing:communicate:** Convey information through an admitted channel. Writing a note is not proof a receiver heard, understood or believed it.
- **wing:compel:** Constrain or redirect another agent's choices. Agency, resistance, permission and unattended decisions must be declared.
- **wing:conceal:** Restrict what particular observers can detect. Must specify the affected senses and conditions; hiding presentation does not change detection.
- **wing:consume:** Spend or eat an available resource. Native stock and feeding rules apply; the catalogue does not select a universal casting resource.
- **wing:convert:** Transform accounted resources with stated byproducts. Native conversions must satisfy declared accounting; this is not unrestricted creation or annihilation.
- **wing:create-or-destroy:** Introduce or remove material under an explicit world rule. Unbound as unrestricted magic: origin, destination, accounting and any permitted breach must be declared.
- **wing:death:** End an individual's current living state. Native death does not settle an edition's death saves, afterlife or continuing-subject rules.
- **wing:detect:** Read a specified feature through an admitted sense. Perception is an observer-specific reading; natural sight exists but no general detection-effect binding is declared.
- **wing:dimensional-access:** Perceive, reach or move along an additional spatial direction. Optional extension: sensory access, bodily extent and movement are independent capabilities; planar travel is not assumed equivalent.
- **wing:dispel:** End or suppress a specified active effect. Needs addressable effects and interference rules; removing a trait cannot safely undo overlapping sources.
- **wing:displace:** Move another bearer by an external force. An actor's voluntary step is not a forced-displacement binding.
- **wing:divide:** Divide a living body or continuing agency. Native budding creates offspring; it does not establish duplicated identity or shared memories.
- **wing:emotion:** Change an individual's emotional or motivational state. A site condition is not an individual's emotion; native needs and beliefs require an explicit binding.
- **wing:enchant:** Attach a lasting, addressable magical change to a bearer. Unbound: requires effect identity, source, bearer, activation, maintenance, expiry, interruption and interference. A timed note is not an enchantment and cannot provide rollback.
  - **ars5:** Investing a device and casting a sustained spell are separate procedures. Laboratory work, resources, triggers and effect frequency are retained as edition-specific requirements.
  - **dnd5-srd51:** A temporary weapon spell and a permanent magic item have different procedures. Preserve target, modifiers, concentration, expiry and dispelling; no common lifetime is selected here.
  - **pf2-player-core:** Runic Weapon's temporary weapon changes and heightened effects belong to that spell. The catalogue does not equate them with permanent runes or another edition's bonuses.
- **wing:flight:** Support controlled movement through open space. A flight label does not establish lift, control, landing, carrying capacity or edition-specific movement.
- **wing:fluid:** Change a fluid's distribution or flow. Resource transfer alone does not implement fluid geometry or motion.
- **wing:fuse:** Combine bodies or establish collective agency. Physical grafting and computational aggregation do not by themselves merge subjects or decision rights.
- **wing:graft:** Attach admitted material or parts to a body. Native compatibility, stock, anatomy and receipt checks still apply; attaching a part does not grant every associated ability.
- **wing:grow-body:** Grow the next parts of an admitted body recipe from paid material. Native growth fills or adds recipe parts and accounts for matter. It does not resize existing extents, replace a stat block, merge equipment or implement temporary polymorph.
  - **ars5:** Transformation guidelines and their limits are separate from native development. Technique, Form, requisites, magnitude, Range, Duration and Target are not inferred from a growth cost.
  - **dnd5-srd51:** These spells specify distinct size or form changes and their own reversions and statistics. Recipe growth is a lasting material process; those procedures require separate bindings.
  - **pf2-player-core:** A battle form's statistics and limitations are edition-native. Paid growth supplies neither battle-form statistics nor temporary size and reach adjustments.
- **wing:heal:** Restore recorded bodily injury. Native healing pays its material cost and repairs lost cells; it does not restore arbitrary hit points or regrow every missing part.
- **wing:illusion:** Change perceived information under a stated sensory rule. Unbound: must identify perceivers, modalities and how interaction can expose the deception.
- **wing:immunity:** Exclude a specified class of incoming effects. A label is not a universal immunity; exceptions and receiver classes remain explicit.
- **wing:incarnate:** Bind a continuing subject to a body. Continuing-subject storage and carryover remain open; birth does not establish incarnation.
- **wing:light:** Change illumination in the world. Native sunlight energy accounting does not by itself create or conceal visible light.
- **wing:locomotion:** Move through the world's admitted places and routes. Site travel, paid in-site strides and an edition's movement budget are different native procedures.
- **wing:modify-check:** Change a check or statistic under stated stacking rules. Native defence has layered readings, but no general edition modifier binding is declared here.
- **wing:pocket-space:** Establish a bounded accessible region with stated spatial relations. The region's topology, contents, entrances and persistence must be declared.
- **wing:portal:** Create an admitted connection between locations. A relation between locations does not itself create a traversable, sensed or persistent portal.
- **wing:possess:** Change the relationship between a subject and bodily agency. Requires explicit control, identity, consent, resistance and termination semantics.
- **wing:regenerate:** Reconstruct missing anatomy. Native healing of lost cells and growth of recipe parts are candidates for a future composed procedure, not universal regeneration.
- **wing:remember:** Acquire, preserve or alter an individual's memory. A historical record is not itself an individual's memory or knowledge.
- **wing:reshape:** Change a body's form while declaring what identity and state persist. Temporary polymorph, permanent transformation and development are separate procedures.
- **wing:resist:** Reduce or oppose a specified class of incoming effects. A named resistance needs its receiver classes, magnitude and interaction rules.
- **wing:resize:** Change existing body dimensions. Native recipe growth does not resize existing extents; support, mass, clearance and reach must be rederived.
- **wing:restrain:** Limit available movement or actions. Constraints must affect the actual movement and choice machinery; a condition label alone is insufficient.
- **wing:resurrect:** Restore life under explicit identity and body rules. Not established by native birth or healing; costs, consent and retained state are separate requirements.
- **wing:reveal:** Make specified information available to an observer. World truth, sensed information and an observer's beliefs remain distinct.
- **wing:shed:** Detach anatomy through a declared developmental process. Severing by injury is not a complete voluntary shedding or metamorphosis procedure.
- **wing:store-effect:** Retain an executable effect for later invocation. The shared function evaluator stores charge; it does not store executable enchantments.
- **wing:summon:** Bring an identified or generated being into an encounter. Spawning a new body does not settle whether the being already existed, obeys or returns elsewhere.
- **wing:sustain:** Maintain a change while its continuing conditions hold. Unbound: needs an addressable effect and declared maintenance and interruption semantics.
- **wing:teleport:** Change location without traversing the intervening route. Native movement follows its travel rules; it does not establish teleportation or destination admission.
- **wing:terrain:** Carve or fill admitted terrain. Native terrain actions still need geometric adjudication, reach and material accounting.
- **wing:thermal:** Change temperature or heat exchange. A generic condition counter does not implement heat transport, freezing, burning or their costs.
- **wing:transfer:** Move a declared resource between accounts. A native transfer moves a named account and cannot translate spell slots into energy without an admitted rule.
- **wing:trigger:** Apply a branch when a declared condition is satisfied. A native When is evaluated within one admitted act; it does not register a persistent event subscription.
- **wing:vow:** Create an obligation with stated terms and consequences. Native agreements exist, but no generic magical enforcement or oath procedure is declared.
- **wing:vulnerability:** Increase susceptibility to a specified class of effects. Native injury and an edition's damage weaknesses need distinct mappings.
- **wing:ward:** Restrict passage or interaction for declared receivers. A ward needs geometry, receiver tests, ownership and a lifetime; an arbitrary trait is insufficient.
- **wing:weather:** Change atmospheric conditions and their duration. Weather needs process, spatial and temporal bindings rather than a presentation effect.
- **wing:wound:** Remove living cells and record bodily injury. Native cell loss is not a hit-point subtraction; defence, target part and overflow rules remain native.
