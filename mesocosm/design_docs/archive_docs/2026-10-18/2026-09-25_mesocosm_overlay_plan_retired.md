# The Mesocosm overlay plan: what moved out on 2026-10-18

**Why this moved.** On 2026-10-18 Mark made the old numbered rulings cases,
kept as precedent and numbered 1 to 984 in the design record, with new rules
numbered by him alone, and asked every active doc to lose its stacked status
banners, old progress entries, stale counters and pasted copies. This file
holds, verbatim, what left the live [Mesocosm overlay
plan](../../2026-09-25_mesocosm_overlay_plan.md): its stacked status banners
and the progress entries that no longer describe open work (the newest, which
do, stay). Case numbers in the moved text are the old ruling numbers, which
the design record and its archive keep.
