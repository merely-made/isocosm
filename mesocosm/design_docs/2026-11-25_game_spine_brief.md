# Mesocosm's spine: one game over four timescales

**Status:** 2026-11-25: design brief, asked for by Mark ("Yes, draft it")
after he named four games as Mesocosm's inspirations. It is not a plan and
changes no code. It holds no rules of its own: it reads the doctrine as one
game, says what each timescale of play is and lacks, and asks what the
doctrine leaves open (§10). Its questions were answered on 2026-11-25 as
cases 1244 to 1251, under §10; the doctrine statements they refine
(Mesocosm 1 and 5, Record 7) are not yet rewritten. Mark's words are quoted
with their date.

**For:** one place that says what Mesocosm is to play, from a moment to deep
time, so building can be judged against a whole game rather than a phase.
It cites the [doctrine](../../design_docs/doctrine.md) by statement and the
plans by phase; where they disagree, they win and this brief is corrected.

## 1. The ask, in Mark's words

Mark, 2026-11-25: "for mesocosm: simant (one of many of a thing,
directing), black & white (or maybe simanimals, but sandbox-ish, generated,
unique critters?) (nesting neural net individuals varying the whole over
evolutionary timescales to develop something like strategies/instinct from
your play), simlife (genotype (genome), phenotype (traits), lineage
(history), individuation (variation upon the theme taking into account
condition), change over time (mutation, genetic drift, ecological niching,
etc.)), simearth (evolve sentient life (though not just one), vary a
planet's conditions/shape, balance the other two parts of the game in the
development of the planet's ecology, model fantastical systems like magic,
gods, physically impossible worlds, etc.). i'm not particular about voxels,
pixels, glyphs, 2, 2.5, 3d, raytracing, tiltshifting, top down, isometric,
side scrolling..., if those games can exist as one. what is the most
plausible, practical way forward to a complete game design that you would
understand how to build from?"

The four are influences, not games to rebuild. The prior-art brief already
sorts them: SimLife and SimEarth as the aggregate tier, Black & White among
gods acting through a creature, Spore as the anti-pattern (several games
glued at their seams), which the founding record's §2 forbids.

## 2. The game in a sentence

You are a participant whose persona the world's lineages come to regard; you
live as one critter of a line at a time, steering it only by attention, and
everything else you come to hold (kin that heed you, the line's next form,
other lineages, the world's own cycles) is earned by what your lines did for
the world and what survived selection.

Each piece is ruled: the placeless participant and its bond (Directing 4),
the nudge as the only directive (Mesocosm 2), regard keyed to the persona
(Mesocosm 2, case 1214), succession (Mesocosm 1 and 3), the edit between
rounds (Mesocosm 6, Ecology 1), world-class holding gated by regard
(lineage-rounds D18). PROJECT_DESCRIPTION's pillars stand; §11 notes where
the description and the doctrine have drifted.

## 3. Four timescales, one lineage

The four games are four timescales of the same world and lineage, not four
modes. The creature minds brief's three learning timescales (local
plasticity, cultural fold, reproduction and boundary) run under the first
three.

| Timescale | Influence | The player's seat | Sim grain | View rung (Presentation 2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A moment: a meal, a chase, a birth | SimAnt | One critter, nudging; kin near it as a cohort | Individuals in the near tier (Directing 5) | Live parts, sprites |
| A life, a round | Black & White | The line through its living members; nudges teach, trust grows per kind of direction | Individuals near, crowd far (Processes 6) | Sprites, marks per individual |
| The epoch boundary | SimLife | The line's next form: the draft and the scored candidates | The forward copy, coarse (EB7, case 1094) | The sheet; aggregate marks |
| Deep time | SimEarth | A world-class lineage, the world's cycles and events, worlds offered by persistence | The coarse step (coarse-step brief), the aggregate | The world map |

The rungs move one way. What the player does at a moment (where attention
keeps leading, which nudges served) becomes a member's learned delta (M3.6),
a third of each parent's delta passes at birth (case 1058), the boundary
selects among lines by reproductive success (Ecology 1), and over deep time
selection works on the rate constants themselves (case 1119, 3.7). That
chain is what makes the timescales one game. **It is unbuilt past M3.5**: the
lineage policy evolves, but members do not yet learn, so play does not yet
reach the next generation.

## 4. Verbs

Every verb is a logged native command or a pick the sim scores; none is a
menu over a body (pillar 1). A finite list, by timescale:

**A moment** (Mesocosm 2, Directing 1, 3 and 5):

- *Nudge*: click a place or a thing; the critter answers by its own lights.
- *Act ring*: right click a target for the acts the body admits toward it.
- *Examine* and *pin*: bring a thing to full detail; keep it attended.
- *Ask*: an in-world ask to a companion (go, hold, fight, carry).
- *Drive*: only a sophont, and only by its agreement.
- *Take*: at a birth, stay with the parent or take the offspring.

**A life, a round** (Mesocosm 1, 3 and 7):

- *Choose a start*: a known lineage, where it lives or one step beyond.
- *Continue*: at a death, an heir from the list (lineage-rounds D4).
- *Speciate*: split the line and name it; *fork* with a plan to compete or
  cooperate with its origin (Ecology 1).
- *End or forfeit*: agree a span, pick a verified condition, offer to end.
- *Steer a colony*: trails, caste shares, calls, the nest (Mesocosm 7).
- *Switch a strain*: gene switches and plasmids at host events (Mesocosm 1).

**The boundary** (Mesocosm 6, Ecology 1, lineage-rounds D2):

- *Draft*: remaps drawn from what the line incorporated, within upkeep.
- *Read the sheet*: needs, abilities, tolerances, as a diff with previews.
- *Pick*: among candidates the forward copy scored, the scores in view.
- *Pick among what prospered*: variants that bred, behaviours that spread,
  what the critter discovered (case 1094).
- *Edit freely*: the creative mode, within the kingdom's bounds.

**Deep time** (Mesocosm 1, lineage-rounds D19, World 4 and 5):

- *Shape conditions*: bounded offsets on temperature, water and light;
  conditions (floods, droughts, blooms) composed from them.
- *Set time's flow and cycles*; answer the events conditions raise.
- *Watch*: any known lineage, or the phase with nobody picking.
- *Found*: a new world, in the editor (World 5).

## 5. A session

What an evening of play is, read from the doctrine. Steps marked † are
unbuilt or unruled.

1. The player opens a world and picks a known lineage and a start
   (Mesocosm 3). A first world offers its founded lines.
2. They live as one critter: it forages by its own choice, they nudge it
   toward food, away from a predator it does not yet fear, toward kin.
   Nudges that serve it grow its bond and its trust in that kind of
   direction.
3. It breeds; they stay or take the young. It dies; they continue as an
   heir. The kin near them have heard them before and heed sooner †(M3.6,
   trust per kind).
4. The round ends by the span agreed or the condition picked †(LR1, EB3).
5. At the boundary they see what prospered, read the sheet, draft from what
   the line ate, and pick among scored candidates †(LR2 to LR4). Every other
   lineage drifts beside theirs.
6. Between rounds, if a world-class lineage regards them, they shape its
   conditions and cycles †(LR8).
7. The next round starts on the new world. Over many rounds lines speciate,
   some become sapient and are driven (Directing 3) †(the measure), and the
   world keeps the record.

**The tempo does not yet fit a sitting.** A round is an epoch, a year by
default (Record 7). The default tick is a minute of world time
(`rules::DEFAULT_TICK_MICROSECONDS`), so a year is 525,960 ticks
(`rules::year_ticks`): about 14.6 hours of play at the head's 10 ticks a
second (sim-execution D21), 73 hours at Mesocosm 5's 2. Nothing in the
records said what a round takes in wall time or how time passes when
nothing attended needs the foreground. Case 1249: time runs at play speed
only while the foreground needs it, aiming at a round of an hour or two.

## 6. Goals and failure

| Timescale | What is at stake | Failure | Ruled by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A moment | The critter's needs, its safety | Death; the trick is the cohort's extra lives | Directing 6 |
| A round | Keeping the line played: young, kin | No heir left in the round: wait it out, offer to end | Mesocosm 3 |
| The boundary | The line's next form | A pick the copy scores badly; the edit lost with nothing surviving | Ecology 1, Mesocosm 3 |
| Deep time | The world's web, the lines you made | Regional collapse (migration), total collapse (watchable, not playable) | Mesocosm 3, Ecology 3 |

Near aims are ruled: verified goals offered per round (EB3), speciation's
reward ladder (EB4, top rungs unspecified), significance (EB5). The long
aim is case 1246: deep time develops lineages until they change the world
("algae, forests, lavos of chrono trigger"), which reshuffles the board and
the resources a line can reach, over an ecological span of billions of
years (unplayed epochs lengthening, 1250). Civilisations are Eponym's and
the VTT's (1247).

## 7. The view follows the grain

The doctrine already gives one camera over every timescale: the 2:1
dimetric in quarter turns (Presentation 1) drawing five rungs from live
parts to the world map, chosen by projected size and what the sim holds,
with examining lifting detail (Presentation 2). The zoom from a critter to
the planet is that ladder, not a mode switch, and what each rung draws is
what the sim runs at that distance: bodies near, marks for individuals,
aggregate marks for bands, the map for the world. Styles per type (2D, 2.5D,
3D projections of the same data, cases 1185 to 1188) cover Mark's "voxels,
pixels, glyphs". A first-person or over-the-shoulder camera is allowed and
not built (Presentation 1, denizen P1). Nothing here needs ruling.

## 8. Fantastical worlds

All of it is ruled (case 1245, Mark: "divinity, effects, there was a glyph
random effect idea where you'd collect 'em and become divine by getting 'em,
ars magica rules, dnd rules, pathfinder rules, etc."):

- **Physically impossible worlds**: a world map in any shape, "a sphere, a
  ring, a world resting on a critter", the critter's acts reaching the world
  as its environment (World 1); worlds in company, forked or neighbouring
  (World 2).
- **The world as an entity**: inert, sentient, a sophont, a god, dead; its
  timeline a lineage, its geology its anatomy, its storyteller its own
  agency (World 4).
- **Magic**: composed cantrips under equivalent exchange, scripts joining
  other generators' characteristics (Processes 17); six axes and composition
  in the functional generation plan, parked.
- **Gods**: the hagioglyph's glyphs, collected and sacrificed for
  divinity, its tier by the journey and its power by the frequency of the
  domain's process (Organs 1 and 2; the wing organs plan), parked under 279.
- **Rules**: one binding language for world rules and rulesets, each world
  choosing whether the sim leads (Schema 8); the editions played faithfully
  (VTT 5), the 5e SRD, Pathfinder 2e and Ars Magica's open text among those
  that qualify (Licensing 1).

Where they meet Mesocosm's play: the world itself is the top world-class
lineage a player may hold (1248). Magic and gods come after v1 (1251): they
add verbs to a loop that must hold without them.

## 9. What each timescale stands on

| Timescale | Built | Missing for play |
|---|---|---|
| A moment | Directing D1 to D5, the switch, native bodies with stances, supported turning and sensed reachable food | Trust per kind, regard (Mesocosm 2); certification of M3 and M4i |
| A round | Lineage policy evolving (M3.5); growth grammar operators (LR6) | Rounds as such (LR1); members learning (M3.6); culture (M3.7); the tier line (E5) |
| The boundary | Boundary, review, revision, speciation native (`lineage/`) | Success-weighted drift (LR2), the draft (LR3), the sheet (LR4), a coarse copy faster than exact (EB7) |
| Deep time | Generated worlds, climate, seasons (`Condition::Season`) | The coarse step (E4b, the coarse-step brief), world-class levers (LR8), a web that holds for years on more seeds (sim S10) |

Lineage-rounds D1 already orders the first slice (LR1 to LR4, solo, on
today's bodies). Read against §3, M3.6 belongs in that slice too: without
it a played round leaves no trace in the next generation, and the loop
plays as SimLife with a camera rather than as one game. Case 1251 adds it to
v1.

## 10. Questions for Mark

1. **The world as a lineage.** World 4 gives the world a lineage's timeline;
   the macro kingdom is a body that carries places (lineage-rounds brief
   §3). Can a player come to hold the world itself?
   - *Recommended:* yes, as the top world-class lineage, held by regard
     like any other (D18), its regard read from the web's health
     (Ecology 3) since World 4 keeps its temperament off its people; its
     levers D19's offsets, time's flow and cycles.
   - World-class means habitat critters only; the planet stays its own
     storyteller and no player holds it.
   - The planet is shaped only at founding and in creative mode.
2. **What a round takes in wall time.** A year at the minute tick is 15 to
   73 hours of play (§5).
   - *Recommended:* time runs at play speed only while something attended
     needs the foreground (an encounter, a meal, a birth, a nudge in flight)
     and otherwise passes faster over the coarse step, aiming at a round of
     an hour or two; the aim set as a measured bar with the speed bars.
   - A shorter default epoch for Mesocosm worlds (a season), the year kept
     as a world setting.
   - A coarser tick for Mesocosm worlds.
   - Rounds span sittings at play speed, saved between them.
3. **The long aim.** What a world is for after a hundred epochs.
   - *Recommended:* no ending; the world's record is the aim (EB5's
     significance, the hagiograph), with per-round goals (EB3) as near aims
     and a lineage turning sapient as the one marked milestone.
   - A stated end: raise a sapient lineage, then the world closes as a
     finished record.
   - A world counts as made when its web holds for an agreed span with no
     one playing (worlds offered by persistence, 1119 §3.7).
4. **Past sapience.** SimEarth runs on to civilisation; ruling 886 keeps the
   society layer off for Mesocosm.
   - *Recommended:* Mesocosm stops at sapience and culture (Directing 3,
     Processes 14); a sapient line's people continue in Eponym or the VTT
     by inheritance under the pipeline laws, never as homework.
   - The society feature turns on in Mesocosm for sapient lineages, late in
     a world (contradicts 886, so asked before anything is written).
   - A world setting chooses.
5. **What a complete v1 is.**
   - *Recommended:* the middle scale's three lifestyles, the solo loop
     (LR1 to LR4) with members learning (M3.6), the world map as a read-only
     view between rounds, and an answer to question 2; microbes, colonies,
     shared lineages, world-class levers, magic and gods after.
   - LR1 to LR4 exactly as D1 ruled, M3.6 after.
   - That slice plus one candidate kingdom, the colony first.

## 11. Surfaced, not edited
