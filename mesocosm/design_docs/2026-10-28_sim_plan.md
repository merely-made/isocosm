## Constraints

- **Derivation.** A world is a seed, its rules and the set of asserted facts; everything else is derived on demand and may be discarded (old §1, Derivation).
- **Tests are draws.** A receipt is a seeded draw from the generator's declared space; a fixture pins a regression and is never a done-condition (ruling 15). Each phase retains its actual done-condition: an executable subset does not close a broader phase (old §9).
- **One definition, two ways.** A process runs step by step for the foregrounded few and as a rate over a distribution for the many, and the two agree: the noted run individually, the fungible agree in distribution (75, 113).
- **Open sets are data.** Kinds of location, forms of governance, kinds of nis, glyphs, magic systems and trait catalogues are authored or generated data, never enums (63, 72, 97, 101).
- **The sim never** renders, takes input, runs a camera or a turn order, knows a hit point, pathfinds an individual, resolves a blow, or decides what is fun (record §3.8).
- **Determinism.** Replay from the seed and the log reproduces the world, collection included, on every peer (104, D11, D32).
- **Trophic stop rules (S8):** no "draw from X / resist Y" affix; payloads act on bodies and never on payloads; no per-organism composition vectors; no fields; no second authority; no constant nudged until the instrument agrees; milligram exactness per channel (ruling 802).
- **Planted faults (society layer):** a society phase is done only when every fault it adds is caught and restored (old stock plan, `archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-10-14_stock_title_plan.md:38-41`).
- **Parking.** Divinity, magic, language, technology and neighbouring worlds are parked: not a priority now, open to design and code (`wing-organs` D1).

#### S10.4. Ecological roles and founding conditions
**Status, 2026-11-04: direction recorded; implementation open.** 2026-11-08: the slice's read-only per-scope and source readings are built (`directing::readings::scopes`, with S10.3 step 3); role queries and founding proportions wait until the recovery criterion is ruled. An authored role may combine known traits into a formula/query over their native facts. Its definition must say what it classifies: available catalogue entries, realized lines or actual living populations. Finer roles and overlapping roles are useful; their overlap must be explicit when allocating a founding mixture, so one founder is not counted or funded twice. A role query reads the world. An empty match reports absence; it does not create a line, grant its traits or change a body's food permissions.

A possible explicitly admitted "found that line" action is a separate constructive operation. A classification formula alone does not supply compatible anatomy, development, reproduction or a reachable resource path. Construction needs a realizable recipe using known capabilities, declared founding matter and other costs, an admitted source and the native validation/conservation path. World generation records its initial budget; a later action must name and pay its actual source. A known catalogue entry is not a living regional donor. The final role syntax, recipe selection and any new action's cost formula remain design questions.

Numeric bands and acceptance thresholds are stated before an experiment; none is chosen by this annotation. Record patch, fixed geographic region and whole-world readings separately, with their actual member sites or patch addresses. Dynamic region membership remains visible beside the fixed comparison footprint. Local or regional disappearance may be turnover within a surviving wider ecology; report it separately from global loss of a line, trait or realized function. A role can persist through line turnover, and a surviving label does not prove that its resource and reproductive functions remain viable.

Recovery must name actual surviving source populations, their location and sufficient native access by movement or dispersal. Connected, disconnected and absent-source controls distinguish actual return from injected replacement.
